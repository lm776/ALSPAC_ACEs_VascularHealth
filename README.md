# ALSPAC_ACEs_VascularHealth

This repository is used to share code used within the analyses of data from the Avon Longitudinal Study of 
Parents and Children, within the study "Adverse Childhood Experiences and Changes in Vascular Health from Childhood 
to Mid-Adulthood: Cross-Sectional and Longitudinal Evidence from the ALSPAC Study".

Study Aims:

1) Assess whether ACEs are associated with PWV, Arterial Distensibility and cIMT measured in adolescence and early adulthood.
2) Determine whether ACE exposure is associated with the level of change in PWV, Arterial Distensibility and cIMT between 
adolescence and early adulthood.
3) Explore whether the relationships between ACEs and vascular health differ by sex, and after adjusting for 
sociodemographic variables, child health risk behaviours and physiological biomarkers.

The study was pre-registered via OSF (view-only link provided: https://osf.io/6gxn8/overview?view_only=1e2e34729194476cb8c74380d653e696)
